GENERATED by `tools/lobo-directives` from `src/config/table.lu` — never hand-edit (the gauntlet regenerates and diffs).

Every directive lobo recognizes has a declared row keyed by (name, context). A directive with no row is a load-time error naming the nearest known row. `named_error` rows are the class-C posture: refused BY NAME, never silently ignored, never accidentally inert.
